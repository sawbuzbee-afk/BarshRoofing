Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a roof replacement cost?

It depends on your roof’s size, pitch, and the shingle tier you choose — from Good (GAF Timberline HDZ®) to Best (designer-class shingles). The only way to get an accurate number is a free inspection, where we measure your roof and give you a straight, written quote.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roofs are torn off, dried in, and fully shingled in a single day. Larger or more complex roofs may take two days. Weather is the biggest factor — we’ll always give you a realistic timeline before we start.

Will you work directly with my insurance company?

Yes. We document hail and wind damage with drone and ground photos, meet your adjuster on site if needed, and make sure nothing gets missed on your claim.

Is the inspection really free?

Yes — no cost, no obligation. We’ll inspect your roof, give you a full photo report, and tell you honestly what it needs, even if that’s nothing right now.

What warranty comes with a new roof?

As a GAF Certified and Owens Corning Preferred Contractor, qualifying installs can be covered for both materials and workmanship for up to 50 years — backed by the manufacturer, not just by us.

Do you serve my city?

We cover the entire Greater Houston area, from Spring and The Woodlands to Galveston and College Station — see our full service area list above, or just give us a call.
