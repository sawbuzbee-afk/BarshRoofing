The Barsh Replacement Process

Most roofs are torn off in the morning and finished by evening. Here’s exactly how it goes.

01

Free Inspection

We assess your roof, document everything with photos, and give you a straight recommendation.

02

Tear-Off

Old shingles come off down to the deck. Landscaping and property are protected with tarps.

03

Deck & Dry-In

Damaged decking is replaced, then premium synthetic underlayment seals the roof watertight.

04

Install

New shingles, ridge, flashing, and ventilation installed to manufacturer spec.

05

Cleanup & Walkthrough

Magnetic nail sweep, full haul-off, and a final walkthrough with your photo report.
